Apprentice engineer, electrical power systems

Toulouse INP-ENSEEIHT (N7), 3EA track, L3 FISA  |  Schneider Electric, HV substations

I am an N7 engineering student and, at the same time, an apprentice at Schneider Electric. After serving in the French Air and Space Force, I went back to school to build a career in electrical engineering: a BTS Électrotechnique in 2026, then the 3EA apprenticeship programme at ENSEEIHT. Two years as an apprentice electrician at Cegelec Défense taught me what a power installation looks like on site, before I moved to the commercial and technical side of high-voltage substations.

What I bring Field experience on critical electrical installations, military discipline, and the technical-commercial skills to turn a customer need into a priced HV substation offer.

400 V three-phase power supply: sizing and installation

BTS project

CFAI de Beauzelle

Situation
BTS Électrotechnique project: supply industrial 400 V three-phase outlets.
Task
Size the installation so that it is safe, selective and compliant.
Action
Wrote the calculation note in Caneco BT (voltage drop, thermal stress, prospective short-circuit current Icc), selected selective breakers, RCDs and cable cross-sections, drew single-line and multi-line schematics in CAD.
Result
A complete, documented design file: calculation note plus schematics.

Electrical refurbishment of two collaborative workspaces

BTS project

Study and on-site work

Situation
Two professional work rooms needed a full electrical overhaul.
Task
Design the new installation, then build it.
Action
Calculated lighting levels (lux), drew up circuit tables and sized the distribution board. On site: mounted raceways, pulled cables, installed sockets and RJ45 network points.
Result
Two rooms refurbished, from study to commissioning. Building plans are not published (NDA).
Lighting designDistribution boardNetwork cabling

Fault diagnosis on production machines

CFA workshops

Hoist axis and automated packaging line

Situation
Practical workshops on machines with real faults.
Task
Find the cause of each fault and restore normal operation.
Action
Hoist axis: traced faults in the drive, the brake holding circuit and the variable frequency drive settings. Packaging line: adjusted vibrating bowl feeders, curing ovens and Ecolregul process regulators.
Result
Machines diagnosed and brought back into service.
